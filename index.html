Straight answers.

Is this actually free?

Yes. No deposit, no invoice afterward, nothing to sign. The photos are what I get out of it, and you keep the right to use those too.

What is the catch?

The catch is that your photos end up inside my app as an example of what a real local business looks like, with your name and business type next to them. That is the price. If it is not something you want, this is not for you and I would rather you skip it than regret it later.

Who owns the photos?

You get a full license to use them anywhere, forever, with no credit required and no expiry. I keep the right to use the same photos in the app and its marketing. Neither of us can stop the other from using them.

Can I say no to a specific photo?

Yes. Look through the set, point at anything you do not want out there, and it is gone from my side as well as yours. No argument, no explanation needed.

Are you going to sell me something after?

Not as part of this. The job is done when the photos are in your hands.

If you like the work and want to talk about more, tell me and I will happily have that conversation. If you do not bring it up, neither will I. We are square.

Why only 2 businesses?

Because I have a job and paying client work, and I would rather shoot 2 places properly than 8 of them badly. If this round fills up, apply anyway and I will come back to you when I open the next one.

Do I need to clean up first?

A little. Sweep, clear the counter, turn all the lights on, park the ugly stuff out of frame. You do not need to renovate. A working shop that looks like a working shop photographs better than an empty showroom.

What if I am outside Fresno and Clovis?

Apply and tell me where you are. I am keeping this round tight so I can actually show up on time, but I keep every application for the next one.
